-:: Lecture note ::-
- aggregate all rows by a specific column. 
- often used with aggregate functions eg. SUM(), MAX(), MIN(), AVG(), COUNT(), etc.
- WHERE clause after GROUP BY dont go well together normally. instead we use the word HAVING, it just do the same thing as WHERE.
- The HAVING clause is used to filter the result of the GROUP BY statement based on the specified conditions.

*** Commands ***
-- syntax
SELECT column_name(s)
FROM table_name
WHERE condition
GROUP BY column_name(s)
HAVING condition
ORDER BY column_name(s);
